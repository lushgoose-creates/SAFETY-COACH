# Lathe Safety Operating Procedure

[SAMPLE CONTENT — replace with the formal SOP provided by your training center]

Rule 1: Students must complete safety training and be authorized by the instructor before operating this lathe independently; do not start the machine without authorization.
Rule 2: Before operating, wear safety goggles, a well-fitted work uniform, and non-slip closed-toe shoes; do not use loose or damaged protective equipment.
Rule 3: Never wear gloves while the spindle, chuck, or workpiece is rotating, to prevent gloves from being pulled into rotating parts.
Rule 4: Long hair must be tied back under a work cap; sleeves must be fastened; do not wear loose clothing, scarves, or dangling jewelry.
Rule 5: Before starting, check that the guards, chuck, cutting tool, and emergency stop are in good condition; do not start if anything is abnormal.
Rule 6: The emergency stop is the red mushroom button on the control panel; in danger, press it immediately, then switch off power and tell the instructor.
Rule 7: The workpiece must be clamped firmly in the chuck or fixture; long bar stock must be supported as required by the instructor; never start with a loose workpiece.
Rule 8: After clamping, remove the chuck key immediately and confirm it is clear of the chuck before starting the spindle.
Rule 9: The cutting tool and tool post must be locked; tool overhang must meet the instructor's requirements; do not use damaged or loose tools.
Rule 10: Guards must be in their required positions; never remove, bypass, or disable any guard or interlock.
Rule 11: Before starting, confirm no person or obstacle is in the working area; run a low-speed test as required by the instructor, then begin machining only after confirming normal operation.
Rule 12: Never touch, measure, or wipe a rotating workpiece; stop the machine fully before doing so.
Rule 13: Never stop the spindle or chuck by hand, cloth, or any other object; use the normal stop and wait until it fully stops.
Rule 14: Clear chips only with a dedicated chip hook; never pull them by hand; stop the machine and wait for rotating parts to fully stop before clearing.
Rule 15: The operator must not leave the machine while it is running; if you need to leave, stop the machine and switch off power first.
Rule 16: If abnormal noise, vibration, burning smell, loose workpiece, or tool damage occurs, stop immediately and report to the instructor.
Rule 17: Before adjusting the workpiece, measuring dimensions, changing tools, or troubleshooting, stop the machine, switch off power, and wait for the spindle to fully stop.
Rule 18: One lathe is operated by only one designated person; others must not operate buttons or approach the rotating area without permission.
Rule 19: Do not lean on the lathe; do not place tools, parts, cloths, or other objects on the machine, chuck, or guide rails.
Rule 20: After machining, stop the machine, switch off power, wait for the spindle to fully stop, then clear chips and the working area.
Rule 21: Keep aisles and the area around the machine clean; report and clean up oil spills promptly; do not stack parts or objects that block evacuation.
Rule 22: If any fault or safety device failure is found, stop using the machine, post a warning, and report to the instructor; never repair the machine yourself.
