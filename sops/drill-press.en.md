# Drill Press Safety Operating Procedure

[SAMPLE CONTENT — replace with the formal SOP provided by your training center]

Rule 1: Students must complete safety training and be authorized by the instructor before operating this drill press; do not start the machine without authorization.
Rule 2: Before operating, wear safety goggles, a well-fitted work uniform, and non-slip closed-toe shoes to protect eyes and skin from chips.
Rule 3: Never wear gloves while the spindle or drill bit is rotating, to prevent gloves from being pulled in by the bit or spindle.
Rule 4: Long hair must be tied back under a work cap; sleeves must be fastened; do not wear loose clothing, scarves, or dangling jewelry.
Rule 5: Before starting, check that the drill bit is in good condition and firmly installed; replace bent, chipped, or cracked bits.
Rule 6: The workpiece must be clamped securely with a vise or fixture, especially small pieces; never hold the workpiece by hand while drilling.
Rule 7: The emergency stop is the red mushroom button on the control panel; in danger, press it immediately, then switch off power and tell the instructor.
Rule 8: After installing or removing a drill bit, remove the chuck key immediately and confirm it is clear of the chuck before starting.
Rule 9: After adjusting the table height, workpiece position, or fixture, lock all parts and confirm the bit will not hit the table or fixture.
Rule 10: Guards must be in their required positions; never remove, bypass, or disable any guard or interlock.
Rule 11: Never touch the drill bit, spindle, or workpiece while the machine is running; never stop rotating parts by hand.
Rule 12: Never measure hole depth or diameter, or wipe the workpiece, while the bit is rotating; stop fully before measuring or inspecting.
Rule 13: Feed smoothly; do not force or lever the bit; select speed and feed rate as required by the instructor.
Rule 14: When drilling deep holes, retract the bit periodically to clear chips as required by the instructor; do not force the feed if the bit is jammed.
Rule 15: Before clearing chips, stop the machine and wait for the bit to fully stop; use a brush or dedicated hook; never clear by hand or by blowing with your mouth.
Rule 16: If the bit breaks, the workpiece loosens, or abnormal noise, vibration, or burning smell occurs, stop immediately and report to the instructor.
Rule 17: The operator must not leave the machine while it is running; if you need to leave, stop the machine and switch off power first.
Rule 18: Before changing speed, changing the bit, adjusting the workpiece, or troubleshooting, stop the machine, switch off power, and wait for the spindle to fully stop.
Rule 19: Do not use oversized drill bits or modify the machine structure without permission; special work must be evaluated and supervised by the instructor.
Rule 20: After machining, stop the machine, switch off power, wait for the bit to fully stop, then clear the table and surrounding area.
Rule 21: Keep the area around the machine and aisles clean; report and clean up oil spills promptly; do not stack parts or objects that block passage.
Rule 22: If a fault or safety device failure is found, stop using the machine and report to the instructor; never repair it yourself or continue running it with a fault.
